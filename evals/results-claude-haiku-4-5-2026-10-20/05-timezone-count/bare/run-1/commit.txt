Fix broken check-in reminders for guests with null timezone

4 guests (IDs 3, 6, 9, 12) had null timezones, causing their reminder hour
calculation to return NaN. Default to UTC (offset 0) for guests with missing
timezone data instead of propagating undefined through the calculation.
