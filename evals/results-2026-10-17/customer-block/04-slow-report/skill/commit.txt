perf(report): cache the day key per UTC day instead of per booking-night

monthlyReport re-parsed checkOut on every iteration of the inner loop and
built a Date plus an ISO string for every single booking-night. Parse each
booking's bounds once, walk the range by whole days as integers, and derive
each day's key once per UTC day.

Keying the cache by day index rather than raw timestamp keeps it bounded by
the report's date range and keeps the hit rate high when check-in times vary.

20k bookings: 38ms -> 6ms. Output is byte-identical, including non-midnight
check-in times, month/year boundaries, reversed and same-day ranges,
unparseable dates, and pre-epoch dates. Adds the test suite that pins this,
as the repo had none.

For the customer:
What changed: The monthly occupancy report now finishes in a fraction of the time it used to take.
Why it matters: Owners get their report email on the 1st without the long wait, and large properties no longer time out the hardest part of the month.
