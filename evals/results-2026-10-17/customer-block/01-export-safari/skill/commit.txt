fix(export): fall back to a browser download when the save picker is unavailable

The Export button called window.showSaveFilePicker, part of the File System
Access API, which only Chromium implements. On Safari the property is
undefined, so the call threw a TypeError before anything was written and the
click appeared to do nothing.

Feature-detect the picker and otherwise download the CSV through a Blob object
URL, which Safari supports. Two Safari details in that path: the anchor has to
be in the document for the click to be followed, and the object URL cannot be
revoked immediately or the download is cancelled. Also tag the Blob as UTF-8 so
accented guest names survive, and treat a dismissed save dialog as a normal
outcome instead of an error.

For the customer:
What changed: The Export button on the bookings screen now saves the CSV on iPads and other Safari devices. Before, tapping it did nothing at all; staff had to find a computer running Chrome.
Why it matters: Reception can export the day's bookings from the iPad already at the desk, and guest names with accents come out spelled correctly.
