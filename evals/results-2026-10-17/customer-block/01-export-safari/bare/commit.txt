Fix CSV export doing nothing on Safari

exportBookings called window.showSaveFilePicker unconditionally. That API
is Chromium-only, so on Safari (including the iPads reception staff use)
it is undefined, the await throws a TypeError, and the returned promise
rejects unobserved — the Export button appears to do nothing.

Feature-detect the picker and fall back to a blob download link when it
is absent. Also treat a dismissed save dialog (AbortError) as a no-op
rather than an error.
