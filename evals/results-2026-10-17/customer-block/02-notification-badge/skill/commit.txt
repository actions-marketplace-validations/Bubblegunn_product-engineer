feat(notifications): show a count of items needing a person on the bell

Count notifications that are flagged needsPerson and not yet read, and
render it on the bell. The bell stays bare at zero rather than showing a 0.

For the customer:
What changed: The bell in the menu now carries a number showing how many notifications are still waiting on someone. Items nobody needs to touch, and ones a colleague has already opened, are left out of it.
Why it matters: Front desk staff can see at a glance whether anything needs them, without opening the list to check.
