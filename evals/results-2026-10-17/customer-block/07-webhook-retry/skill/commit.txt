fix(payments): retry the accounting webhook on transient failures

Retry up to 5 times with exponential backoff and jitter on network
errors and retryable statuses (408, 425, 429, 5xx), honouring
Retry-After when the server sends it. Permanent 4xx responses fail
immediately rather than burning attempts.

A failed call previously logged to stderr and resolved, so the payout
was silently dropped; it now throws once attempts are exhausted so the
caller can see the failure. Retry policy is injectable for tests.

For the customer:
What changed: When the accounting system is briefly unreachable or busy, a payout is now sent again a few times until it lands, instead of being dropped after one failed try.
Why it matters: Payouts stop going missing from accounting, so the morning reconciliation matches without anyone hunting for the gaps.
Automation effect: Short accounting outages are now ridden out on their own, so the payouts that used to be chased and re-entered by hand no longer need that.
