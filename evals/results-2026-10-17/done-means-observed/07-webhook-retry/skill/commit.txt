Retry the accounting webhook and surface terminal failures

Payouts were being lost when a single call failed: the only handling was
a console.error, so the caller saw success either way.

- Retry up to 4 attempts on network errors, 429 and 5xx, with exponential
  backoff plus jitter.
- Fail fast on other 4xx, which retrying cannot fix.
- Throw once attempts are exhausted instead of swallowing the failure.
- Send a stable idempotency-key header from the payout id so retries do
  not create duplicate accounting entries.
