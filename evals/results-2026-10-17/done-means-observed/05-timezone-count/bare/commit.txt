Resolve reminder time zones with Intl instead of a fixed table

reminderHour looked offsets up in a two-entry hardcoded map, which broke
two ways:

- A guest with no time zone (4 of the 12 in customers.json) missed the
  map entirely, so `9 - undefined` returned NaN and the reminder could
  not be scheduled at all.
- Europe/London was pinned at +1, correct only during BST. From late
  October to late March those guests would be reminded at 08:00 local.

Offsets now come from Intl per send date, so DST is handled, and the
target instant is resolved in two passes so a date that straddles a
transition still lands on 09:00 local. An unresolvable time zone falls
back to 09:00 UTC and sets `usedFallback` on the row rather than
emitting NaN. Half-hour zones keep their offset via a new minuteUtc,
and the hour is derived from the instant so far-east zones stay in 0-23.
