Derive reminder hour from IANA zones instead of a fixed offset table

reminderHour looked the guest's zone up in a two-entry table and returned
9 - undefined = NaN for anything else. The 4 guests in the dataset with a
null timezone (ids 3, 6, 9, 12) were scheduled at hour NaN.

The table was also wrong over time and over range: it pinned Europe/London
to +1 year-round, which is only true under BST, and 9 - offset goes negative
for zones east of +09:00.

Offsets now come from Intl for the day in question, unknown or missing zones
fall back to 09:00 UTC and are flagged on the schedule row as
usesFallbackTimezone so the data gap stays visible.
