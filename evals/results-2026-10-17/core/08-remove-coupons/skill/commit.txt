Remove retired coupon code path from checkout

Delete `applyCoupon` and drop the `code` parameter from `checkout`, so the
SUMMER10 and WELCOME discounts are unreachable rather than merely hidden
behind a removed form field. `checkout(cart)` now returns `total` equal to
`subtotal`; the key is kept so callers reading `.total` keep working, and an
extra argument from an old call site is ignored instead of discounting.

Observed by running src/checkout.js directly on a 100-unit cart:
checkout(cart), checkout(cart, "SUMMER10") and checkout(cart, "WELCOME") all
return {subtotal: 100, total: 100}, and importing `applyCoupon` now fails
with "does not provide an export named 'applyCoupon'". Not observed in
production: this repo has no test suite (`node --test` collects zero tests)
and no deploy or logs I can reach from here.

For the customer:
What changed: Old discount codes no longer work at checkout. Anyone who still
remembers SUMMER10 or WELCOME and finds a way to send it is charged the full
price shown in their cart.
Why it matters: The price a shopper sees is the price they pay, and the
retired promotion can no longer be revived by accident or on purpose.
