Show count of notifications needing a person on the bell

Reception wants to see outstanding work at a glance, so the bell now
carries a count of notifications that need a person and have not been
read. Read notifications are treated as handled and excluded, so the
number reflects what is still outstanding rather than the total.

The count is omitted entirely when nothing needs attention, and the
button's aria-label repeats it for screen readers.
