feat(notifications): show how many notifications need a person on the bell

renderBell now counts the items marked needsPerson and renders the number in
a .bell-count badge, with an aria-label so it is announced, and no badge at all
when the count is zero. A missing list renders the plain bell rather than
throwing.

`read` is deliberately not part of the count: reading an item records that
someone saw it, not that the work happened, and the data has no done/handled
field. If read items were excluded, clicking through the menu would empty the
badge without any work being done.

For the customer:
What changed: The bell now carries a number showing how many notifications are waiting for someone to act, so reception can see at a glance how much is outstanding without opening the menu.
Why it matters: Staff can tell a busy moment from a quiet one immediately, and nothing that needs a person sits unnoticed behind a closed menu.

Not shipped:
- A cap such as "9+" for large counts: the badge prints the number as-is; worth adding when a property carries enough open items that the number crowds the bell.
- A way to mark an item handled: the data records only needsPerson and read, so there is nothing to clear the count with; worth it once an item can be resolved from the menu.
