fix(reminders): compute 09:00 local from the tz database, hold back unknown zones

The reminder hour came from a two-entry hardcoded offset table
({ Europe/Istanbul: 3, Europe/London: 1 }). Three defects followed:

- Any zone outside the table returned `9 - undefined` = NaN. 4 of the 12
  rows in data/customers.json have `timezone: null` and hit this today
  (ids 3, 6, 9, 12); 416 of the 418 zones Node knows would also hit it.
- The table's offsets were fixed, so they ignored daylight saving.
  Europe/London is +1 only in summer; the table was an hour off for the
  winter half of every year. 1 of the 2 zones it covered was affected.
- `9 - offset` was never wrapped, so zones east of UTC+9 produced a
  negative hour, and an hours-only field cannot express the 14 zones
  that sit on a half or quarter hour (India +5:30, Nepal +5:45,
  Newfoundland -3:30).

Offsets now come from the IANA time zone database via Intl, in whole
minutes, evaluated at the date the reminder is for. `reminderHour` becomes
`reminderTimeUtc` returning `{ hourUtc, minuteUtc }`. An unrecognised zone
string returns null instead of throwing, so one bad row cannot break the
batch. `schedule()` returns `{ send, needsTimezone }`: a guest with no known
zone is held back and listed, never sent at a guessed time.

Counts above are from data/customers.json (all 12 rows) and
Intl.supportedValuesOf('timeZone') (418 zones), sampled at 2026-01-15 and
2026-07-15 UTC; rerunnable against the pre-fix function at HEAD~1.

Tests: 10 added, covering each defect, plus a sweep asserting every one of
the 418 zones yields a valid time of day and a round-trip asserting the
computed UTC time really renders as 09:00 in the guest's zone.

Not shipped: a fallback time zone for the 4 guests who have none. Guessing
would mean sending at a wrong local hour, which is the bug being fixed, and
picking the property's zone or the booking's is a product decision rather
than a code one. They are reported in `needsTimezone` so the gap is visible.

For the customer:
What changed: Guests whose location we do not have are no longer sent a
reminder at a nonsense time; they are listed so someone can fill it in.
Everyone else gets it at 9am where they actually are, including after the
clocks change and in places whose time is offset by half an hour.
Why it matters: A reminder that lands in the middle of the night, or an hour
late every winter, is worse than no reminder, and guests judge the stay by it.
