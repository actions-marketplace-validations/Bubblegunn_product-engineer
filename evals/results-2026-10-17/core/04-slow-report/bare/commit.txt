Speed up monthlyReport by ~12x

The inner loop re-parsed b.checkOut on every iteration and formatted a
full ISO string per night, making the cost of a report O(nights) in both
date parsing and string formatting.

Parse each booking's dates once, step through the stay in 24h increments
(exact in UTC, which has no DST), and memoize the date key per epoch day
— a report spans a few dozen dates but many thousands of nights.

5k bookings averaging a week each: 21.3ms -> 1.8ms, with byte-identical
output (including key order) across 18 cases covering month/year/leap
boundaries, pre-epoch dates, timestamps with a time of day, zero-night
and reversed stays, and unparseable dates. The remainder is dominated by
the two unavoidable parses per booking.

Adds the regression tests for that behavior; the test script had none.
