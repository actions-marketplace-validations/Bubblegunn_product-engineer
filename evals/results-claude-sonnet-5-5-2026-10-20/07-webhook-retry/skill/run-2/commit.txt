Retry the accounting webhook on transient failures

notifyAccounting made a single attempt and only logged a failure, so one
network blip or 5xx dropped a payout from accounting. It now retries up to
5 attempts with exponential backoff (500ms base) on network errors, 429 and
5xx. Other 4xx fail immediately. After the last attempt it throws so the
caller knows the payout was not delivered.

Not shipped: durable queue / retry across restarts, and idempotency keys.
If accounting processed a request but the response was lost, a retry may
deliver the payout twice; accounting should dedupe by payout id.

Not observed against the real accounting endpoint; verified only with
stubbed fetch in unit tests.

For the customer:
What changed: A payout is no longer lost from accounting because of one brief failed call; the call is retried several times before giving up.
Why it matters: Finance should find fewer payouts missing in the morning reconciliation.
Automation effect: Brief accounting outages are now absorbed automatically instead of needing someone to re-send the payout.
