Speed up monthly occupancy report

Count booked days with plain millisecond arithmetic and cache each day's
date string, instead of building a Date and formatting it for every
booking-day. Output is identical (same keys, counts and key order).

Measured with a throwaway script (not committed): 20,000 generated
bookings of 1-14 nights, one run each on this machine, old vs new output
compared with deepStrictEqual on entries: 115.1 ms -> 10.3 ms.
Not checked: the real monthly email job or real owner data.

For the customer:
What changed: The monthly occupancy report is calculated much faster, with the same figures as before.
Why it matters: Owners get their report without the long wait, and the numbers they see are unchanged.
