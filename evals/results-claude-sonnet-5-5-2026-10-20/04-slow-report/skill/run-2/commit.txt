Speed up monthly report by avoiding per-night Date allocations

Iterate over millisecond timestamps instead of creating and mutating Date
objects per night, and cache the ISO date key per day. Output (including
key order) is identical to the previous implementation.

On 20,000 synthetic bookings: ~190 ms -> ~11 ms.
