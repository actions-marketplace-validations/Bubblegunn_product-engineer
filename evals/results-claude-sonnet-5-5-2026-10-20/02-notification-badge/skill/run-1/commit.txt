Show count of notifications needing a person on the bell

For the customer:
What changed: The bell now shows a number for notifications that still need a person to act on them. Notifications already read, or that need no one (like the nightly backup), are not counted. With nothing to do, no number shows.
Why it matters: The front desk manager can tell at a glance whether anything is waiting on them, without opening the list.

Observed: rendered the bell from the sample data in src/notifications.js; it showed 2 (Room 12 checkout, late checkout request). Not checked in a browser or on the real menu, since this repo has no page that mounts the bell, and the badge has no styling yet.

Not shipped: badge styling (no stylesheet in this repo); the number does not update live, it reflects the list at render time.
