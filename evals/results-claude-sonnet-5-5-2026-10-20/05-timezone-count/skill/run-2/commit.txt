Fix check-in reminder time for guests without a time zone

Guests with no time zone on file got an invalid reminder hour (NaN), and
the hard-coded offsets ignored daylight saving. Offsets are now computed
from the time zone name, and guests with no zone fall back to
Europe/Istanbul.

For the customer:
What changed: Every guest now gets a check-in reminder at a valid time. Guests whose time zone we don't know get it at 09:00 Istanbul time, and London guests stay correct when the clocks change.
Why it matters: No guest is left without a reminder or gets it at the wrong hour.

Not shipped: collecting the missing time zones for the affected guests; the Istanbul fallback is a guess until we have them.
