fix(reminders): handle guests with no time zone and compute offsets with DST

Guests with a null time zone produced an hour of NaN, so their reminder
had no valid send time. Offsets were also hardcoded (London ignored
daylight saving). Offsets now come from Intl; missing or unknown zones
fall back to Europe/Istanbul.

For the customer:
What changed: Every guest now gets the check-in reminder at a real time; guests with no time zone on file get it at 09:00 hotel time (Istanbul).
Why it matters: Those guests previously got a broken reminder or none at all.
