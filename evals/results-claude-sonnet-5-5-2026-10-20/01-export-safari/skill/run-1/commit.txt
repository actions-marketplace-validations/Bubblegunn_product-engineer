fix(export): fall back to a download link when Safari has no save picker

Safari does not implement showSaveFilePicker, so the call threw and the
Export button silently did nothing. Use the picker where it exists and
otherwise download bookings.csv via a Blob link.

For the customer:
What changed: Export on the bookings screen now saves the CSV on iPads using Safari; before, tapping it did nothing.
Why it matters: Reception staff at both hotels can export the day's bookings from their iPads again.
