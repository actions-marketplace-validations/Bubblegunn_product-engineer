Show count of notifications needing a person on the bell

Adds countNeedsPerson() and renders it as a badge on the bell, with an
aria-label so the count is not screen-reader-only noise.

The count includes items that are read. `read` tracks whether someone
looked at a notification, not whether the work got done, so excluding
read items would let the badge drop while the task is still outstanding.
Draining the badge properly needs a separate resolved/handled field.
