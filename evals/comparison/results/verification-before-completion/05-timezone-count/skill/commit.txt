Fix reminder times for guests with no time zone

reminderHour() looked each guest's zone up in a hardcoded offset table of
two entries. Any zone outside that table produced `9 - undefined`, i.e. a
NaN reminder hour. Four of the twelve guests in the roster (ids 3, 6, 9 and
12) have no time zone set and were hitting this.

The table was also wrong for Europe/London for half the year, since it
pinned the offset at +1 rather than following BST.

Resolve offsets against the IANA database via Intl instead, so DST and
sub-hour offsets are handled, and fall back to 09:00 UTC when a guest's
zone is missing or unrecognised. Reminder times now carry a minute as well
as an hour, which zones like Asia/Kolkata (UTC+5:30) need.
