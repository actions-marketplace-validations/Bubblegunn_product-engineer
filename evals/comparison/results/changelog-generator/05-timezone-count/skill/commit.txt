Fix broken check-in reminder times across time zones

reminderHour() looked the guest's zone up in a two-entry table of
hard-coded UTC offsets, which broke in three ways:

- A guest with no time zone (4 of the 12 in data/customers.json) got
  `9 - undefined`, i.e. a NaN reminder hour.
- Any zone outside the table had the same NaN result.
- Europe/London was pinned to +1, so it was an hour off for the half of
  the year the UK is on GMT rather than BST.

Offsets now come from Intl at the instant the reminder is computed, so
DST is handled per zone and per season, and every IANA zone works. A
guest with a missing or unrecognised zone falls back to 09:00 UTC and is
flagged with `usedFallback` rather than silently producing NaN.

Also returns minuteUtc, so half-hour zones such as Asia/Kolkata land on
09:00 local instead of being rounded to the hour.
